Us. A Diary for Two

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Us is a private daily diary and scrapbook for two people, made by Petalform LLC ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what data the Us app collects, why, who processes it on our behalf, and the choices you have. We built Us to be private by default: there is no public feed, and your entries are visible only to you and the partner you invite, except for a page you choose to publish with a share link.

Who we are

The data controller is Petalform LLC. For any privacy question or request, contact us at hello@heyus.app.

Data we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressTo create and secure your account and to send account emails such as verification.
Name or display nameSo you and your partner can recognize each other inside the app. Optional.
Your entries: diary text, notes, prompt answersThese are the content of the app. We store them so you can keep and revisit them.
Photos you addStored so they appear on your pages and in your diary.
Purchase and subscription statusTo unlock and maintain Us Plus. Payments are processed by Apple or Google, not by us.
Push notification tokenTo deliver nudges and reminders, if you enable notifications.
App activity and diagnosticsBasic in-app events and crash reports, so we can keep the app working and fix problems.
Device identifiersA device or installation identifier used by our diagnostics and analytics tools.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or content from other apps. Our diagnostics and analytics are configured to strip personal content and identifiers such as message text, names, and email addresses before events are recorded.

How your data is shared

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. We use a small number of service providers who process data only on our behalf, under contract:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage (your account and entries).
  • RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements.
  • Sentry for crash and error diagnostics.
  • PostHog for privacy-scrubbed product analytics.
  • Apple and Google for in-app purchases and push notification delivery.

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

Your partner and shared pages

Us is built for two people. Content you save to a shared space is visible to the partner you invite. If you publish a scrapbook page and share its link, anyone who has that link can view that page until you unpublish it. Everything else stays between the two of you.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access to stored data is restricted, and each account can read only its own records.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data and entries from our systems, except where we must keep limited records to meet legal or accounting obligations.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete specific content at any time inside the app (remove a photo, a note, or a page).
  • Delete your account and all associated data from the app, or by request. See Delete your account and data.
  • Turn off notifications in the app or your device settings.
  • Access or correct your data by contacting us at hello@heyus.app.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. We honor those rights. Write to us and we will help.

Children

Us is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
